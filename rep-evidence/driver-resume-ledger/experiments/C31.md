# Experiment: C31 — the redo window is deterministic once the producer blocks

**Pre-registered:** 2026-09-10, iteration 20, before the arms were written.

Three claims have now been made about the same number and two are dead:

- **C27** — `redo = Q·W + 2W` exactly. Refuted by one point: `Q=16` gave 53.
- **C30** — the last term varies, so `Q·W + 2W` is a loose upper bound. Refuted
  by six repeats at `Q=8` returning **30 every single time**.

Both refutations were pre-registered outcomes, and together they force a third
reading: the formula is right, and the `Q=16` measurement was taken in the wrong
*state*.

Reconstructing that arm: durable 45 + queue 48 + writer 3 = 96, against 98
executed. So `p = 2`, meaning the producer was **not blocked in `q.put`** — it
was still executing units, with the third unit of its batch in flight. Reaching
the blocked state costs `Q·W + W` units after the outage begins: 27 units at
`Q=8` but **51** at `Q=16`, which at the measured throughput lands almost exactly
on the +30 s death mark.

**The defect is my negative control.** It asserted `queued == Q`. A full queue is
necessary for the producer to be blocked but **not sufficient** — the producer
can have just completed a `put` that filled the queue and moved on to produce
again. I verified the buffer was full and inferred, wrongly, that the pipeline
was stalled.

## Claim

- **C31.** Once the producer is *actually blocked* on a full queue, the redo
  window is exactly `Q·W + 2W`, deterministically and independently of when the
  crash lands. A smaller redo means the producer had not yet blocked — not that
  the bound is loose.
  *Refutes if:* a death taken well after the producer blocks yields anything
  other than `Q·W + 2W` at any `Q`, **or** the `Q=16` arm still yields 53 when
  its death is delayed past the blocking point.

## Arms — an A/B on the same `Q`

| arm | `Q` | outage | death at | predicted redo |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `q16_early` | 16 | 55 s | **+30 s** | **53** — reproduces the anomaly |
| `q16_late` | 16 | 55 s | **+45 s** | **54** = `Q·W + 2W` |
| `q32_late` | 32 | 55 s | +45 s | **102**, *only if* it blocks in time |

`q16_early` is the negative control and the design of this experiment rests on
it. It must reproduce **53**. If the anomaly does not reproduce, then it was
noise, C27 was refuted by noise, and this whole line of reasoning collapses —
the run would be **VOID**.

`q32_late` is included as a deliberate stress: `Q=32` needs `32·3+3 = 99` units
to block, which at the measured rate may **not** fit inside a 55 s outage. It is
predicted to come out *below* 102 for exactly the reason `q16_early` does. It is
not evidence for the formula; it is a second instance of the trap, and predicting
the trap in advance is the point.

## The control that was missing

Redo is now recorded together with a **blocked** flag derived from the
coordinator's own state at the instant of death: the producer is blocked iff
`executed − durable == Q·W + W + W`, i.e. `p == W`. Each arm reports it, and only
arms with `blocked == True` may be used as evidence for the formula.

## Prediction (frozen)

1. `q16_early` reproduces **53**, `p=2`, `blocked=False`. Confidence 0.6 — the
   phase is timing-dependent, so it may land on 54 by luck; if it does, the run
   is inconclusive rather than void, and needs repeating at a shorter delay.
2. `q16_late` gives exactly **54**, `p=3`, `blocked=True`. Confidence 0.85.
3. `q32_late` gives **less than 102** with `blocked=False`. Confidence 0.6.
4. All arms contiguous, no duplicates.

## What each outcome means

| `q16_early` | `q16_late` | reading |
|---|---|---|
| 53 | 54 | **C31 proven.** One formula, one state condition, three claims resolved. The design number is `Q·W + 2W` and `Q` must be small enough to block within the outage. |
| 54 | 54 | inconclusive — the anomaly did not reproduce; retry with an earlier death |
| 53 | ≠54 | **C31 refuted.** The formula is wrong even in the blocked state. |
