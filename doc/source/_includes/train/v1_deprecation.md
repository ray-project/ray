:::{warning}
Ray Train V1 is deprecated and will be removed in Ray 3.0, no earlier than May 2027. Ray Train V2 is the default starting in Ray 2.51. If you use V1 by setting `RAY_TRAIN_V2_ENABLED=0`, see the [Ray Train V2 migration guide](https://github.com/ray-project/ray/issues/49454).
:::
